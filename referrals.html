How you're paid

Monthly, alongside my own billing cycle. By bank transfer as standard — or in USDT or BTC if you'd prefer, at the prevailing rate on the day.

If the reward rate changes

These percentages may change from time to time. If you've already introduced a client before a change, the schedule that applied on the date of that client's Order Form is honoured for the full 12-month reward period — it doesn't drop or rise mid-way through.

The one rule I have to follow

As an SRA-regulated solicitor, I'm required to tell any client in writing when someone has referred them and is being paid for it. That's not optional on my end — it's a standard part of the client care information for that engagement, and it protects the person you've referred by making sure they know the introduction wasn't entirely disinterested. It doesn't affect your reward, and it's a formality most people barely notice.

Who this works for

Anyone — a founder who's already a client, a friend at another company, a fellow adviser on Connectd or elsewhere who sees businesses that need this kind of help but isn't the right fit to advise them on it themselves. No limit on how many introductions you make.
