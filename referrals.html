Referrals

Know someone who needs a lawyer like this?

Simple version: introduce someone, and if it turns into real work, I say thank you properly.

£150, once the person you introduce becomes a client and their spend with KeepCounsel passes £1,000. No cap on how many people you introduce.

How it works

Email hello@keepcounsel.io and introduce me to whoever you have in mind — or just tell them to mention your name when they book a call. Once their spend with KeepCounsel passes £1,000, I'll send you £150, by bank transfer, no questions asked.

If you're already on a KeepCounsel retainer yourself, that reward doubles to £300 — same process, same no-questions-asked payout.

The one rule I have to follow

As an SRA-regulated solicitor, I'm required to tell any client in writing when someone has referred them and is being paid for it. That's not optional on my end — it's a standard part of the client care information for that engagement, and it protects the person you've referred by making sure they know the introduction wasn't entirely disinterested. It doesn't affect your £150, and it's a formality most people barely notice.

Who this works for

Anyone — a founder who's already a client, a friend at another company, an adviser or investor who sees a lot of businesses that need this kind of help. There's no limit on how many introductions you make.
