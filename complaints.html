If something goes wrong

Every engagement is covered by professional indemnity insurance. Our complaints procedure is set out in full within your client engagement letter, provided at the start of every instruction. In short: raise it with us directly first, and if it isn't resolved to your satisfaction, you have the right to refer the matter to the Legal Ombudsman (service complaints) or the Solicitors Regulation Authority (conduct concerns, such as discrimination).
