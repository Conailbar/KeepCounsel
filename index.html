Example catch — the cap that doesn't cap

"Each party's total liability shall not exceed the Fees paid in the preceding 12 months, for claims under this Agreementfor claims under this Agreement or under any indemnity given by that party."

AI drafts routinely leave indemnity clauses outside the liability cap without saying so. A cap that looks watertight can still leave you exposed to unlimited liability through an indemnity elsewhere in the same contract — a gap that only shows up if you check how every clause interacts, not just how each one reads on its own.
About

Conail Barugh, Principal Solicitor

My career in the legal industry spans 15 years, the last eight as a qualified solicitor advising venture-backed technology and SaaS businesses — from both in-house and private practice.
