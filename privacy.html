Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026.

1. Who we are

This policy explains how Charlie Bravo Services Limited, trading as KeepCounsel ("we", "us"), collects, uses and protects personal data when you visit this website, get in touch, or become a client. We are the data controller for the personal data described here.

2. What we collect

  • Contact details you provide via forms, email or phone (name, email, phone number, company).
  • Information you share about your legal matter when requesting advice.
  • Basic technical data (browser type, device, approximate location, pages visited) via privacy-respecting analytics, if enabled.
  • Booking details (name, email, selected time) if you schedule a call via our Cal.com booking page — handled under Cal.com's own privacy terms as well as this policy.

3. How we use it

  • To respond to enquiries and provide legal services under agreed engagement terms.
  • To meet legal, regulatory and professional obligations.
  • To improve this website and, where you've opted in, to send occasional updates.

4. Legal basis

We rely on: performance of a contract (delivering instructed legal services), legal obligation (e.g. AML/regulatory compliance), legitimate interests (responding to enquiries, improving our service), and consent (optional marketing communications, where applicable).

5. Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for advertising. We use a small number of service providers to run the practice and this website. Each acts under its own terms and, where applicable, a data processing agreement:

  • Zoho Mail — email for hello@keepcounsel.io, including correspondence about your matter.
  • Zoho WorkDrive and Zoho Writer — storage of documents and working files, and drafting or marking up documents.
  • FormSubmit — delivers the contact and AI Contract Review forms on this site to our inbox, including any document you upload with them.
  • Cal.com — booking and managing introductory calls.
  • Stripe — card payments. Stripe receives your payment details directly; we never see or store your full card number.
  • Tide — our business bank account, for payments made by bank transfer or Direct Debit.
  • OpenSign — electronic signature, where a document is signed that way.
  • Anthropic (Claude) — an AI assistant used to support parts of our work. Client documents and personal data are only put into it where doing so is consistent with our confidentiality duties.
  • Cloudflare — hosts and delivers this website, and sees the IP address of every request as part of doing so.
  • Google Analytics (Google) — website usage statistics. This loads only if you accept analytics cookies; see Cookies below.

We may also disclose information where we are required or permitted to do so by law or by our regulator, including to the Solicitors Regulation Authority, the Legal Ombudsman, or a court.

We will update this list as the tools we use change. If you would like to know more about how a particular provider handles your data, ask us and we will point you to their terms.

6. Where your data is held

Some of the providers listed above are based outside the United Kingdom, or hold data outside it. Where personal data is transferred outside the UK, it is protected by the safeguards permitted under UK data protection law — typically the UK International Data Transfer Agreement, the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, or a finding of adequacy for the country concerned.

If you would prefer that a particular document is not sent through this website’s upload form, email it to hello@keepcounsel.io instead, or ask us to arrange another method.

7. Retention

Client file data is retained in line with our professional and regulatory obligations (typically 6 years from the end of a matter). Enquiry data that does not proceed to instruction is retained for a shorter period and then deleted.

8. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your personal data, and to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact hello@keepcounsel.io. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

9. Cookies

This site does not set any cookies until you choose to. On your first visit, a banner asks whether you're happy for us to use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. If you accept, Google Analytics loads and sets cookies to distinguish visitors; if you reject, or don't respond, none are set and nothing is tracked beyond basic, anonymous server logs.

You can change your mind at any time using the "Cookie preferences" link in the footer of any page. No personal data is sold to third parties for advertising purposes, and Google Analytics data is used only to understand overall site usage, never to identify you individually.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy: hello@keepcounsel.io.
