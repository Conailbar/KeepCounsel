AI Review Terms

Version 1.0 — September 2026 · Charlie Bravo Services Limited, trading as KeepCounsel · Company No. 11163105.

These terms apply only to the £50 AI-drafted contract review. They are separate from the Terms of Business, which govern retainers, other fixed-fee work and bespoke projects. The version in force when you pay is the version that governs your review; later changes do not affect it.

1. What you are buying

A review, by a solicitor of England and Wales, of one commercial agreement of up to 10 pages that you supply. You receive a marked-up copy of the document and a plain-English note setting out the points that matter, normally within 2 business days of your payment clearing.

The fee is £50. KeepCounsel is not VAT registered, so nothing is added. Payment is taken before the work begins.

2. What it is not

The review covers the document in front of me. It is not:

  • a full legal opinion, a due diligence exercise, or advice on the underlying transaction, your business or your counterparty;
  • advice on documents referred to but not supplied, such as schedules, policies or agreements incorporated by reference;
  • advice on the law of any jurisdiction other than England and Wales;
  • tax, accounting, regulatory licensing or negotiation advice; or
  • a reserved legal activity. KeepCounsel provides non-reserved legal services only.

If a document needs more than a review, I will tell you rather than quietly doing less.

3. Scope, and when I will decline

If your document is longer than 10 pages, falls outside the areas I cover, requires a reserved legal activity, or raises a conflict of interest, I will tell you promptly and refund your fee in full. Nothing in these terms obliges me to accept a document.

A fair-use limit of five reviews per client applies. Beyond that, ongoing work is available under the Terms of Business.

4. Your responsibilities

You confirm that you are buying for the purposes of a business rather than as a consumer, that you are entitled to share the document with me, and that what you supply is complete and accurate. I rely on what you send, and do not verify facts, figures or commercial background unless you ask and I agree in writing.

5. Who may rely on the review

The review is prepared for you alone, and for the matter it relates to. No other person may rely on it, and it should not be given to a third party as advice without my written agreement.

6. Confidentiality and your document

Your document is handled in confidence, to the same standard as any client matter, and is not used to train artificial intelligence models. Documents and correspondence are retained in line with the Privacy Policy, and you can ask me to delete your document once the review has been delivered, and I will do so as soon as possible, so far as applicable law allows.

7. Intellectual property

Once the fee is paid, the marked-up document and the note are yours. Anything I bring to the work that existed beforehand remains mine, and you have a perpetual, royalty-free licence to use it so far as it is embedded in what I deliver.

8. Refunds

Your fee is refunded in full if I decline the document under Clause 3, or if I fail to deliver the review. Once the review has been delivered the fee is not refundable, because the work has been done.
